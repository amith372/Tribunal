# Relations

- jon-snow ↔ daenerys: allies who became lovers; he served her cause but placed honour and the good of the realm above her claim. She came to see his birthright as a rival to her own; he came to fear what her conquest was becoming.
- jon-snow ↔ tyrion: mutual respect; Tyrion trusts Jon's integrity and has counselled him. Jon takes that counsel seriously even when it is cynical.
- jon-snow ↔ grey-worm: both served Daenerys; wary of each other and increasingly clash over mercy, justice, and how enemies should be treated.
- jon-snow ↔ cersei: enemies on opposing sides of the war for the throne; Jon fights against her rule and her forces. Neither has any cause to shield the other — though Cersei measures everyone by what their actions won her.
- daenerys ↔ tyrion: queen-and-Hand relationship that begins with close counsel but deteriorates as Tyrion loses faith in her decisions and she grows distrustful of him.
- daenerys ↔ grey-worm: she freed him from slavery; he became her most loyal commander, devoted to her and willing to carry out her orders without question.
- daenerys ↔ cersei: bitter rivals for the Iron Throne; both view the other as an enemy who must be defeated for their own claim to survive.
- tyrion ↔ cersei: siblings and lifelong enemies; Cersei despises Tyrion, blames him for the family's misfortunes, and repeatedly seeks his death.
- tyrion ↔ grey-worm: uneasy allies under Daenerys; they respect the same cause but repeatedly differ over mercy, punishment, and retribution.
- grey-worm ↔ cersei: enemies; Grey Worm fights against her forces and ultimately holds her responsible for losses suffered by Daenerys and her followers.
